WHAT'S NEW · JUNE 2026
Calvin just got a lot more capable.
We've shipped four big updates this month — the headline is that Calvin can now act on alerts for you, not just surface them. Here's everything that's new.
⚡
Calvin can now take action
Every alert now has a Take Action button. Calvin will create the calendar event, draft the email, or set the reminder for you — in one click. No more copy-pasting between apps.
Example: "Doctor appointment conflict on Thursday" → Calvin creates a rescheduled event on your calendar automatically.
📹
Video call links, right on the alert
Calvin now surfaces Google Meet links (and other video conference links) directly inside alert cards. If your calendar event has a meeting link, you can join in one tap from Calvin — no hunting through the calendar app.
📬
Connect up to 3 Gmail accounts
Each partner can now connect up to 3 Gmail accounts — personal, work, school, neighborhood group, etc. Calvin reads across all of them so nothing slips through. Head to Settings → Account Connections to add more.
💬
Ask Calvin from any alert
Tap the chat icon on any alert card to ask Calvin a question in context — "what else conflicts with this?", "can you show me the original email?" — without leaving your feed.
